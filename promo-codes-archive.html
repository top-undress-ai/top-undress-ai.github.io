How We Verify Codes

Every code is redeemed on two brand-new accounts (one on iOS, one on Android) before we publish. We record the credit balance before and after redemption, screenshot the confirmation, and archive the timestamps on GitLab. Full methodology in our testing methodology page.

Why Codes Expire

Undress AI providers rotate promo codes for three reasons: to close abuse loops (mass account creation), to time promotions with product launches, and to segment marketing channels. A code that worked last month is almost never revived — providers issue fresh codes instead. That is why we archive expired ones and refresh the working list weekly.
